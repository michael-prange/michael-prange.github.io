# AI PicScrub Privacy Policy

*Last updated: November 2, 2026*

AI PicScrub does not collect, store or share any personal data.

**Photos.** When you choose photos, AI PicScrub reads them on your device to find and remove AI-provenance metadata. If you save cleaned copies, they are added to your Photos library. Your photos are never uploaded or sent anywhere.

**Photo library access.** AI PicScrub asks for access to your Photos library only to read the photos you select (including their date and location, so cleaned copies keep their place in your library) and to save cleaned copies. You can change this at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Photos.

**Purchases.** The one-time unlock is sold through Apple's App Store. AI PicScrub contacts the App Store only to show the unlock's price and to complete or restore a purchase. Apple handles the payment; the developer receives no personal information about you.

**No other network use.** AI PicScrub doesn't connect to the internet for anything else. It contains no analytics, advertising, tracking or third-party services, and it has no accounts.

**Children.** AI PicScrub collects no data from anyone, including children.

**Changes.** If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.

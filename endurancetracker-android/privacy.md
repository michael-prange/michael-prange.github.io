# EnduranceTracker for Android Privacy Policy

*Last updated: November 10, 2026*

EnduranceTracker keeps your rides on your phone. The developer does not collect, receive or have access to any of your data. There are no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking.

**Location.** EnduranceTracker uses your phone's location to show where you are on the map and, while you record a ride, to record your track — including with the screen off, during a ride you started. Recorded rides are stored on your phone.

**Heart rate.** If you pair a Bluetooth heart-rate monitor, its readings are recorded with your ride and stored on your phone. EnduranceTracker connects only to the monitor you pair.

**Maps and terrain.** Map images are downloaded from the U.S. Geological Survey's public map services, and elevation data for correcting a ride's total ascent from Amazon Web Services' public terrain tiles. These requests say which area of the map is needed, as any map does; they carry no account or personal information. Place search, when you use it, sends what you type to your phone's built-in geocoding service (usually Google's).

**Photos and files.** EnduranceTracker reads only the photos and files you choose — a photo of a trail map to calibrate, a map PDF, or rides to import — and keeps what you import on your phone.

**Voice questions in Help.** If you tap Ask by Voice, your phone's speech-recognition service turns what you say into text; EnduranceTracker receives only the text.

**Google Drive sync (optional).** If you turn on Sync with Google Drive, you choose a Google account with Android's own account picker; EnduranceTracker never sees your password. It asks Google for one permission only: to store its own data in a private app folder in your Google Drive (the `drive.appdata` scope). It cannot see or change any of your other Drive files. Your rides, your horse names and a list of rides you have deleted are stored there, so your other Android devices can sync them. This data goes directly from your phone to your Google Drive; it never passes through or is stored by the developer. It is used only to sync your rides, and is never sold, shared or used for advertising. You can turn sync off at any time in Settings, and remove the stored data from Google Drive (Settings › Manage apps in Drive on the web) or revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

EnduranceTracker's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.

**Sharing.** Nothing leaves your phone unless you share it yourself — for example a ride as GPX or PDF, a backup file, or the diagnostic log you choose to send the developer when reporting a problem.

**Children.** EnduranceTracker collects no data from anyone, including children.

**Changes.** If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.

# EnduranceTracker for Android

EnduranceTracker records endurance and trail rides: your track on a USGS topographic or satellite map, coloured by gait, with distance, time, speed and climb as you ride. It keeps a history of every ride by horse, works with a Bluetooth heart-rate strap and watches for signs of fatigue, and shows your own trail maps — photos of paper maps or georeferenced PDFs — under your track. Maps can be downloaded ahead of time for riding without signal.

Rides can optionally sync between your Android devices through your own Google Drive.
